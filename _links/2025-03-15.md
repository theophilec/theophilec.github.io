Yesterday, espressoinsight shared their Scroll buddy on Hacker News:

> Instead of a boring scrollbar thought it would be fun to have an animated stick figure that walks up and down the side of your page when you scroll.

And it looks really nice!

I asked Claude 3.7 Sonnet to turn the walking stick figure into a bicycle riding down the screen, giving the current webpage in the context. After two re-prompts, this is what we get:

![Claude 3.7 Sonnet scrolling down](assets/video/bicycle-scroll.mov)

I also gave OpenAI o1 on the task. After three tries and some help from me, its version still does not scroll!

If you follow the LLM space, you've certainly heard about Simon Willison's "pelican riding a bicycle" _de facto_ benchmark. Perhaps we can make this a new benchmark?
